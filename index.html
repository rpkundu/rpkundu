Rajendra P. Kundu is an economist based at Jawaharlal Nehru University (JNU) in New Delhi, where he serves as a professor at the Centre for Economic Studies and Planning within the School of Social Sciences. His academic work focuses on applying economic theory to understand and analyze both formal/informal institutions (including legal institutions and social/economic/political networks).
