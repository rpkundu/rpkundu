Rajendra P. Kundu is an economist based at Jawaharlal Nehru University (JNU) in New Delhi, where he serves as a professor at the Centre for Economic Studies and Planning within the School of Social Sciences. His academic work focuses on applying economic theory to understand and analyze both formal and informal institutions (including legal institutions and social, economic, and political networks).
